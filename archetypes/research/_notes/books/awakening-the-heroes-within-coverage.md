## 3. Врезки глав (полнее таблицы) + «Levels of the X» (Shadow / Call / Level 1–3) — VERBATIM
Для каждого архетипа: **Box** = Goal / Fear / Response / Task / Gift; **Levels** = Shadow / Call / L1 / L2 / L3.

**Innocent (гл.6, Ego/Preparation).** Box: Remain in safety / Abandonment / Deny or seek rescue / Fidelity, discernment / **Trust, optimism, loyalty**. Levels — Shadow: Denial, repression, blaming, conformity, irrational optimism, risk taking; Call: safe, secure environment, desire to be protected, unconditional love; L1: unquestioning acceptance of authorities, dependence; L2: the «fall» — disillusionment, but retained faith; L3: Return to Paradise as a Wise Innocent, trust without denial or dependence.

**Orphan (гл.7, Ego/Preparation).** Box: Regain safety / Exploitation, victimization / Powerlessness, wish for rescue, cynical compliance / Process pain and disillusionment fully and be open to receive help / **Interdependence, empathy, realism**. Levels — Shadow: cynicism, callousness, masochism/sadism, using the victim role to manipulate; Call: abandonment, betrayal, disillusionment, victimization; L1: acknowledge the plight and feel pain/powerlessness; L2: accept the need for help, be willing to be rescued; L3: interdependence with others against authority; realistic expectations.

**Warrior (гл.8, Ego/Preparation).** Box: Win, get own way, make a difference through struggle / Weakness, powerlessness, impotence, ineptitude / Slay, defeat, or convert it / High-level assertiveness; fighting for what really matters / Courage, discipline, skill. Levels — Shadow: ruthlessness, obsessive need to win, power for conquest, difference as threat; Call: confrontation of a great challenge; L1: fight to win (anything goes); L2: principled fight, fair rules, altruistic intent; L3: assertiveness for what really matters, win/win, honest communication.

**Caregiver (гл.9, Ego/Preparation).** Box: Help others; make a difference through love and sacrifice / Selfishness, ingratitude / Take care of it or those it harms / Give without maiming self or others / Compassion, generosity. Levels — Shadow: suffering martyr; devouring parent; guilt-tripping; enabling; Call: responsibilities requiring care of others; L1: sacrificing own needs, rescuing; L2: self-care so caring enriches not maims, «tough love»; L3: generativity, community building. (В прозе — ещё 4-й уровень: «positive Martyr», в врезке его нет.)

**Seeker (гл.10, Soul/Journey).** Box: Search for a better life or a better way / Conformity, becoming entrapped / Leave it, escape, take off / Be true to a deeper or higher truth / Autonomy, ambition. Levels — Shadow: excessive ambition, perfectionism, pride, inability to commit, addictiveness; Call: alienation, dissatisfaction, emptiness; opportunity knocking; L1: exploring, wandering, experimenting; L2: ambition, climbing, becoming the best; L3: spiritual searching, transformation.

**Destroyer (гл.11, Soul/Journey).** Box: Growth, metamorphosis / Stagnation or annihilation; death without rebirth / Be destroyed by it, or destroy it / Learn to let go, turn it over, accept mortality / Humility, acceptance. Levels — Shadow: self-destructiveness (drugs, suicide) and/or destruction of others (murder, rape, defamation); Call: pain, suffering, tragedy, loss; L1: confusion, grappling with death/loss/pain; L2: acceptance of mortality and relative powerlessness; L3: choosing to let go of what no longer supports life/growth.

**Lover (гл.12, Soul/Journey).** Box: Bliss, oneness, unity / Loss of love, disconnection / Love it / Follow your bliss, commit to what you love / **Commitment, passion, ecstasy**. Levels — Shadow: jealousy, envy, obsessive fixation, sexual addiction, Don Juanism, promiscuity, or conversely puritanism; Call: infatuation, seduction, yearning, falling in love; L1: following your bliss; L2: bonding and commitments; L3: radical self-acceptance giving birth to the Self.

**Creator (гл.13, Soul/Journey).** Box: Creation of a life, work, or new reality of any kind / Inauthenticity, miscreation, failure of imagination / Accept it as part of the Self; be willing to create another reality / Self-creation, self-acceptance / **Creativity, identity, vocation**. Levels — Shadow: creation of negative circumstances; obsessive creation, workaholism; Call: daydreams, fantasies, flashes of inspiration; L1: opening to receive visions/inspiration; L2: knowing what you really want to create; L3: experiments with creating what you imagine.

**Ruler (гл.14, Self/Return).** Box: A harmonious and prosperous kingdom (life) / Chaos, loss of control / Find its constructive use / Take full responsibility for your life; express your deeper Self in the world / **Sovereignty, responsibility, competence**. Levels — Shadow: controlling, rigid, tyrannical, manipulative; the ogre tyrant; Call: lack of resources, harmony, support, order; L1: responsibility for your own life/family; L2: skills and structures for your group/community; L3: using all resources for society or the planet. **Расхождение таблица↔глава: дар в таблице с.10–11 — «Responsibility, control».**

**Magician (гл.15, Self/Return).** Box: Transformation of lesser into better realities / Evil sorcery (transformation in a negative direction) / Transform or heal it / Alignment of Self with cosmos / Personal power. Levels — Shadow: evil sorcerer/wicked witch, negative synchronicity; Call: illness, or extrasensory/synchronistic experiences; L1: experiencing healing or noticing synchronicity; L2: grounding inspiration, making dreams real; L3: mastery of changing physical reality by changing mental/emotional/spiritual.

**Sage (гл.16, Self/Return).** Box: Truth, understanding / Deception, illusion / Study, understand, or transcend it / Attainment of knowledge, wisdom, enlightenment / **Skepticism, wisdom, nonattachment**. Levels — Shadow: cut off, unfeeling, «ivory tower», critical/pompous; Call: confusion, doubt, deep desire to find truth; L1: search for «the Truth» and objectivity; L2: skepticism, truth seen as relative, acceptance of subjectivity; L3: experience of ultimate truth; wisdom.

**Fool (гл.17, Self/Return).** Box: Enjoyment, pleasure, aliveness / Nonaliveness / Play with it or play tricks on it / Trust in the process; enjoyment of the journey for its own sake / Joy, freedom, liberation. Levels — Shadow: self-indulgence, sloth, gluttony, irresponsibility; Call: boredom, ennui, desire for more enjoyment; L1 (Fool): life as a game; L2 (Trickster): cleverness to trick, get out of trouble, tell truth; L3 (Wise Fool/Jester): life lived fully in the moment, one day at a time.

## 5. Часть V — дополнительные модели (кратко)
- **Гл. 18 — шесть пар жизненных стадий:** Безопасность — Innocent/Orphan (Divine Child); Идентичность — Seeker/Lover (Promised Land); Ответственность — Warrior/Caregiver (Archetypal Parent); Аутентичность — Destroyer/Creator (Rebirth); Власть — Magician/Ruler (World Redeemer); Свобода — Sage/Fool (Enlightenment). Архетипы не привязаны к возрасту жёстко.
- **Гл. 19 — гендер:** мужской путь начинается с «too much Ego» (гордыня), женский — с «too little Ego» (смирение); женщины ведут Lover/Caregiver, мужчины — Seeker/Warrior; зрелость → андрогинность.
